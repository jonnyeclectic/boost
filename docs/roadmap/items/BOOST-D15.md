<code>doctor</code> is an undifferentiated wall of green checks — no hierarchy, no headline. Reframe it as a panel with a verdict line (<code>● Healthy</code> in green / <code>● Attention</code> in amber), grouped checks, dim sub-detail, and severity color so a problem actually stands out instead of hiding among the ✓s.
